ToolVisuals extensions for the bundled Sodium 0.9.3-beta.1+mc26.3 release

The nested upstream Sodium JAR is unmodified. Its license and pinned artifact
metadata are provided alongside this notice.

assets/sodium/shaders/include/globals.glsl and
assets/sodium/shaders/blocks/block_layer_opaque.vsh are derived from the matching
Sodium release (https://github.com/CaffeineMC/sodium). The upstream shader layout,
vertex decoding, texture and lighting paths retain Sodium's license:
sodium-fabric-0.9.3-beta.1+mc26.3-LICENSE.md (PolyForm Shield 1.0.0).

ToolVisuals adds chunk movement/easing in the vertex shader and two parameters
in the existing std140 padding. Separate mixins supply their values and preserve
animation start times across replacement mesh uploads.

assets/sodium/lang/ru_ru.json is a ToolVisuals Russian translation of the option
keys present in that pinned Sodium release.
